# City v2 product visual pass

The approved mathematical building roles, construction-site status, complex memberships and dependency data are unchanged. The Three.js facade generator is reused directly from [AEROVIEW Coast 3D](https://github.com/trinhtanphat/aeroview-coast-3d/blob/85124ca1666ad890810db0d3f39c8aecbd5616f4/src/coastal-visuals.js), MIT, copyright (c) 2026 trinhtanphat. The full licence is in `site/assets/licenses/aeroview-mit.txt` and is staged with the site. Golden palette adapted from its `free-city-visuals.js`; no geographic data or external imagery is imported. The product shell uses a full-width canvas, floating controls and collapsible inspector, with a close district opening, street markings and landscaping. It also wires the previously missing search and inspector elements. Existing overview remains available. Browser visual acceptance and GPU performance still require verification. The current default C.4 is unchanged.

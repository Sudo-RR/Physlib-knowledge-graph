# Architectural rendering pass

The previous visual pipeline stretched 256x512 window textures across whole towers, used low anisotropic filtering and capped default render resolution at device pixel ratio. All overview towers use coarse geometry. Metallic materials had no environment to reflect, and facade emission was strong in daylight. This pass replaces Geneva facades with 1024x1024 modular tiles (world-scale repeating floors/bays based on instance transform), aligned emissive textures, roof masking, a locally generated sky reflection environment and instanced contact shadows. Semantic subject tint is retained at lower strength; geometry, picks and graph IDs unchanged. Dusk raises window emission separately. Medium/high supersample with pixel-count caps; low preserves cheap rendering. Adaptive overview remains enabled, high quality still has distinct geometry thresholds. These are procedural architectural materials, not photogrammetry or physically ray-traced city lighting. The sky is LDR, contact shadows are approximations. Shader compatibility and visual acceptance need browser evidence; CI adds shader-error capture and high-quality screenshots. No external assets or licenses added. Further work: inspect screenshots at normal and focused scales, then replace repetitive building silhouettes with curated low-poly PBR models and introduce bounded real shadows if needed.

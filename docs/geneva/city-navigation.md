# City navigation pass

Restores classical camera-relative WASD movement, hold-to-move with frame time, arrow left/right orbit, arrow up/down zoom, Q/E alternative orbit, pan drag, right/Shift-drag orbit, pinch zoom, and on-screen turn controls. Pitch remains fixed oblique; map is north-up and arrows pan there. Switching map/city retains yaw, zoom and focus. Map marker shows camera centre and heading. Double-click a selected 3D building focuses it; Details opens sidebar on demand. Sidebar moved inside viewport so top controls and bottom selection actions are unobstructed, fixing the previous Chromium failure on Clear Selection. The city canvas remains full width. Dusk, full detail, resolution and architectural baseline unchanged. Key release, blur and visibility reset movement, idle still event-driven. Unit and browser tests cover navigation deltas, pose changes, sidebar overlay and map selection. Physical touch devices and GPU frame rate remain unverified.

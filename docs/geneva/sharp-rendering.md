# Sharp rendering, without architectural redesign

The rejected urban massing commit 6bf872c is reverted to the previously deployed 5f03518 visual baseline. This restores exact original source height mapping, footprints, streets, civic density, camera framing and pitch, waterways, materials palette, window repetition, sky environment and contact shadows. No real shadow pass or new infill remains. High quality and full geometry now default; adaptive and lower quality remain selectable. High resolution targets 2.5x CSS pixels at DPR 1 and up to 3x native DPR, bounded by 8.5 million pixels. Facades are rendered at 2048x2048 with the original 1024 logical drawing coordinates; maximum anisotropy raised to 16 (device-clamped). Round tower and roof meshes use more segments. This deliberately increases GPU load and texture memory; no GPU FPS or photorealism claims. Browser CI captures initial sharp default and still checks adaptive versus full geometry, shader errors, idle redraw and navigation. No changes to mathematical source data.

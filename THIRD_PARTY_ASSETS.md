# City art and third-party assets

The building facades, roof shapes, entrances, trees, roads, signs and ground
colours are generated by this repository's code. No third-party 3D models,
textures, fonts or image files are included in the city art kit. The four roof
variants are visual decoration; they do not denote a Lean declaration type,
construction status, project completeness or importance.

The renderer uses Three.js, an MIT-licensed software dependency; see its
installed package licence and `package-lock.json` for the pinned version.
If external art is added later, record its author, source, licence, any
redistribution conditions and required attribution here before shipping it.

Construction sites are not displayed: the current graph has no verified
construction-status field. A scaffolded appearance must not be assigned from
file size, folder name or guesswork.
